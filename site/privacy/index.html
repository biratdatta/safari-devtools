Privacy

Updated 2026-10-20 · 2 min read

Safari DevTools is built to keep your data on your machine. This page explains exactly what happens to it.

The tool and the extensions

  • Safari DevTools runs inside the page you're looking at. It doesn't send page content, logs or network data anywhere.
  • Your settings for each site (theme, panel position, mock rules, snippets) are stored in that site's local storage in your browser.
  • The browser extensions ask for permission to run on websites so they can add the panel to pages. They don't collect or transmit browsing data.

Optional features that send data

Explain with Claude and Jira bug reports only work if you start the relay on your own computer with your own Anthropic and Jira credentials. When you use them, the prompt or report you see and confirm is sent from your relay to Anthropic or to your Jira site, under your accounts and their terms. Sign-in codes, tokens, cookies and authorization headers are removed first.

Remote mode sends the phone's console and network data to the relay running on your computer, over your local network.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts.
  • The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect it.
  • The live demo runs entirely in your browser.
